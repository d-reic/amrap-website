FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is my data shared publicly?

No. Your journal stays on your phone. Social is opt-in: only the results you choose to share are uploaded.

Do I need an account?

Not for your journal or the timers. You only sign in, with Apple or email, if you want to follow friends, join your box or take part in challenges.

Is there a subscription fee?

Your first 10 entries are free. After that, a subscription lets you log without limits and supports development. Timers are always free.

Can I take my data with me?

Yes. Export and import your journal any time.

What devices does the app support?

AMRAP runs on iPhone with iOS 18 or later.

Email amrap@danielreichenpfader.com.
