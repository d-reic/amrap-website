FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is my data shared publicly?

No. Your journal stays on your phone. Social is opt-in: only the results you choose to share are uploaded, and only your followers and your box can see them. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Do I need an account?

Not for your journal, the timers or warm-ups. You only sign in, with Apple or email, to use AI coach or cloud backup, follow friends, join your box or take part in challenges.

Is there a subscription fee?

Your first 10 entries are free. After that, Premium lets you log without limits, unlocks AI coach and supports development. Timers and warm-ups are always free.

Can I take my data with me?

Yes. Export and import your journal any time, or turn on the optional cloud backup when you’re signed in and restore it on a new iPhone.

What does AI coach see, and who runs it?

AI coach is powered by Anthropic’s Claude. When you ask a question, your message and the parts of your journal it needs to answer are sent through our server to Anthropic. Nothing is sent until you’ve agreed in the app, and AI coach never saves to your journal without asking you first. See the Privacy Policy for details.

What devices does the app support?

AMRAP runs on iPhone with iOS 18 or later.
