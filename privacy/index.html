Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

This policy explains what personal data the AMRAP iOS app (“AMRAP”, “the app”) and this website process, why, and what you can do about it. AMRAP is made by Daniel Reichenpfader in Switzerland (“we”, “us”), who is responsible for the processing described here. Questions: amrap@danielreichenpfader.com.

The short version

  • Your training journal stays on your phone. It is only uploaded if you turn on Cloud Backup, which is off by default.
  • Social is optional. If you sign in, we store your profile and only the results you choose to share, on servers in Zurich, Switzerland.
  • AI coach is optional. When you ask it something, your message and the journal entries needed to answer are sent to Anthropic’s Claude through our server. We don’t store the conversation, and Anthropic does not train on it.
  • Apple handles payments. RevenueCat tells the app whether you have a subscription. We never see your card details.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking across apps or websites. We don’t sell your data.
  • You can delete your cloud backup or your Social account in the app at any time, and everything tied to it is deleted with it.

1. Data that stays on your device

Your journal (exercises, results, weights, times, reps, notes, how the last rep felt, favorites), your timer history and your settings are stored on your iPhone. We have no access to them unless you turn on Cloud Backup (section 4). They are included in your device backups if you use iCloud Backup or a computer backup, which Apple and you control. Use Export Entries in Settings to save a copy as a file; deleting the app deletes this data from the device.

2. Exercise library

To keep the built-in exercise and benchmark library up to date, the app downloads it from our server. Like any internet request, this sends your IP address and basic technical information (such as the time of the request) to our hosting provider, Supabase. No account or personal data is needed for this, and we don’t use these logs to identify you.

3. Social account (optional)

You only need an account to use Social: following friends, kudos, boxes, leaderboards and challenges. If you create one, we process:

  • Sign-in data: your email address if you sign in with a one-time email code, or the identifier and email address Apple gives us if you use Sign in with Apple (this can be a private relay address if you choose “Hide My Email”).
  • Profile: your username, display name, optional profile picture and the box you joined.
  • Shared results: for each result you choose to share: the exercise name and type, reps, weight, time, Rx or scaled, whether it was a PR, your note, and the date.
  • Activity: who you follow, kudos you give, boxes you create, and challenges you create or join.

Who can see what. Your username, display name, profile picture and box are visible to every signed-in AMRAP user, and your profile picture is stored at a publicly accessible web address. Your shared results and their kudos are visible to you, to anyone who follows you, and to members of your box. Following someone does not require their approval. Challenges are visible to their creator, their participants and members of the box they belong to. Leaderboards and challenge progress are calculated only from shared results.

Nothing from your journal is uploaded unless you share that result or turn on Cloud Backup.

4. Cloud Backup (optional)

If you are signed in to Social, you can turn on Cloud Backup in Settings. It is off by default. While it is on, the app uploads a copy of your journal so you can restore it after reinstalling the app or on a new iPhone:

  • Journal entries: exercise, type, date, reps, weight, time, Rx or scaled, how the last rep felt, your notes, and whether you deleted the entry.
  • Custom exercises you created, and which built-in exercises you marked as favorites.

Your timer history and settings are not backed up. The backup is linked to your Social account and stored with Supabase in Zurich, Switzerland. Access rules on our servers make it readable and changeable only by your account; it is never shown to other users or used for leaderboards, challenges or anything else. It is sent over encrypted connections but is not end-to-end encrypted, so, as the operator, we could technically access it; we only do so where needed to keep the service running or when you ask us to.

Turning Cloud Backup off stops further uploads. To remove the copy, tap Delete Cloud Backup in Settings; deleting your Social account also deletes it. Restore from Backup only adds entries that are missing on your iPhone and never deletes anything on your device.

5. AI coach (optional)

AI coach is an in-app assistant that answers questions about your training and can suggest entries for your journal. It is part of Premium, requires a Social account, and asks for your consent before first use. It uses Claude, an AI model by Anthropic, PBC (USA).

  • What is sent: the messages you write in the conversation, the coach’s replies so far, and the parts of your journal a question needs (for example your results for one exercise). The coach asks for specific journal data and the app sends only that; your whole journal is not uploaded.
  • How it is sent: from the app to our server at Supabase, which passes it on to Anthropic’s API and returns the reply. The request is linked to your account only to enforce a daily limit.
  • What is stored: the conversation is kept on your iPhone only. Our server does not store messages; it keeps, per account and day, the number of requests and the amount of text processed (tokens), to enforce the daily limit and monitor costs. These counts are deleted with your account.
  • At Anthropic: Anthropic processes the request on our behalf to generate the reply and does not use it to train its models. Under its commercial terms, Anthropic may keep API inputs and outputs for a limited time for safety and abuse monitoring before deleting them. See Anthropic’s privacy policy.
  • Changes to your journal suggested by AI coach are only saved after you confirm them, and only on your iPhone.

AI replies can be wrong. Don’t share health information or anything else you don’t want processed this way; AI coach is not medical advice.

6. Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple through the App Store. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives a random app user ID, your purchase and subscription status, and basic device and app information (such as the iOS and app version and the App Store country). It does not receive your name, email address or payment details, and we don’t link it to your Social account. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy and Apple’s privacy policy.

7. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA), which processes visitors’ IP addresses to deliver the pages and logs them for security. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and icons from jsDelivr, which receive your IP address when your browser requests those files. The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics. If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you.

8. Why we process your data (legal bases)

  • To provide the app and the Social features you sign up for (performance of a contract).
  • To keep a backup of your journal while Cloud Backup is on, which you can withdraw at any time by turning it off and deleting the backup (your consent).
  • To answer your questions in AI coach, which you agree to before first use and can stop at any time by no longer using it (your consent).
  • To publish the results you choose to share, which you can withdraw at any time by unsharing the result or deleting your account (your consent).
  • To keep the service secure and working, for example through server logs (legitimate interest).
  • To meet legal obligations, for example for bookkeeping (legal obligation).

We process data in line with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where it applies, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

9. Service providers and transfers

  • Supabase (Supabase, Inc.): sign-in, database and file storage for Social and Cloud Backup, the exercise library, and the server that relays AI coach requests. Data is stored in Supabase’s Zurich, Switzerland region.
  • Anthropic (Anthropic, PBC, USA): generates AI coach replies.
  • RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA): subscription status.
  • Apple: App Store purchases and Sign in with Apple.
  • GitHub (GitHub Pages), Google (Google Fonts) and jsDelivr: this website.

Some of these companies are based in the USA and may access data from there. Where data leaves Switzerland or the EEA, we rely on adequacy decisions (such as the Swiss-U.S. and EU-U.S. Data Privacy Frameworks) or the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses. We don’t share your data with anyone else unless the law requires it.

10. How long we keep data

Social data and your cloud backup are kept until you delete them or your account. When you delete your account in the app’s Social tab, we delete your sign-in data, profile, profile picture, cloud backup, AI coach usage counts, shared results, follows, kudos, challenge memberships, and the challenges and boxes you created. Server logs are kept by our providers for a limited time for security and then deleted. On-device data stays until you delete it or the app.

11. Your rights

You can ask us for access to your data, a copy in a portable format, correction, deletion, or restriction of processing, and you can object to processing or withdraw consent at any time. Much of this you can do yourself in the app: edit your profile, unshare a result, leave your box, delete your cloud backup, or delete your account. For anything else, email amrap@danielreichenpfader.com. We will answer within 30 days.

You can also complain to a data protection authority: in Switzerland, the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC); in the EU, the authority in your country.

12. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). On our servers, access rules ensure that you can only change your own data and only see what others have made visible to you. No system is perfectly secure, so please choose what you share with care.

13. Children

AMRAP is not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

14. Changes

We will update this policy when the app or the law changes, and change the date at the top. If a change materially affects how we use your data, we will let you know in advance, for example in the app.
